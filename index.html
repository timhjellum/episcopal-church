The Challenge

The Episcopal Church's quarterly liturgical and educational resources — readings, prayers, curriculum, activities, and supporting materials — lived across fragmented, manually managed distribution with no shared structure. Every quarterly release meant re-solving the same problem: how to get the right resources to the right audience, reliably, on a recurring cycle. I was brought in to architect and build a content management platform from the ground up, replacing ad hoc distribution with a single source of truth.
